I spent years behind a camera. When AI image tools showed up, I wanted nothing to do with them.

Then I actually tried using what I know.

I started referencing specific lenses, specific lighting setups, designing shot lists the way I would for a real production. Except the "production" was a conversation with AI.

The output wasn't generic slop. It was genuinely good. Not because the AI is magic. Because I know what a 35mm does to a product shot versus an 85mm. I know what soft directional light does to texture.

All those years behind a camera didn't become irrelevant. They became the thing that made the output great.

And here's what really hit me. Small businesses have never been able to afford a proper production shoot. Now a restaurant in San Miguel can have beautiful lifestyle imagery for their menu and website. A local boutique can have product photography that looks like it came from a real studio.

The creative eye didn't become obsolete. It became the prompt.
