# LinkedIn Post — WhatsApp AI for Mexican Businesses

---

I was sitting in a restaurant in San Miguel the other day watching the owner's daughter answer WhatsApp messages on two phones.

I asked her how many she gets a day. She laughed.

"Maybe 200. Same questions. What time do you close. Do you have space Saturday. Can I see the menu. How do I get there."

She's been doing this every day for three years. Four hours a day, minimum. While also running the floor, managing staff, and trying to have a life.

Here's the thing. In Mexico, WhatsApp isn't a messaging app. It's the entire business. Customers don't go to your website. They don't fill out a form. They don't send an email. They open WhatsApp and start talking. The whole journey happens there. Discovery, questions, negotiation, payment, follow-up. One thread.

90% of Mexico uses it daily. And almost nobody is automating it with AI.

Not with a basic chatbot. I mean real AI that understands voice notes — because Mexicans don't type, they send audios. AI that switches between Spanish and English mid-conversation because a tourist just wrote "oye, do you have disponibilidad for Saturday?" AI that generates an OXXO payment reference and sends it in the chat because not everyone has a credit card.

We built her a system. Three weeks. n8n orchestrating the workflows, Claude handling the conversations, Whisper transcribing the voice notes.

80% of messages handled automatically by the end of month one. Her team only touches the conversations that actually need a human now.

And the part that surprised me most: customers didn't mind talking to AI. They preferred it. Because it responded in 30 seconds instead of 45 minutes.

The businesses in Mexico that figure this out now are going to have a massive advantage. Because right now the bar is answering WhatsApp manually at 11 PM. And that bar is about to move.
