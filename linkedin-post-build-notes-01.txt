PERSONAL ACCOUNT (linkedin.com/in/semolik/)

I told my MIT professor that AI was broken. He told me to build the house.

A year ago I was a creative who thought in mood boards, not code. I didn't know what an API was. But I could feel AI changing my industry, so I enrolled in MIT Sloan's AI strategy program and started trying to build things on my own.

It was a mess. ChatGPT kept dragging me in circles. Nothing I built worked. I felt like I was assembling furniture with the wrong instructions in a language I didn't speak.

Then my professor said something that stuck: "We gave you the scaffold. Now you build the house."

That's what this series is about. Not tutorials. Not advice. Just an honest account of what happens when a creative person decides to stop watching from the sideline and starts touching the buttons.

First post is live.
