## Tactical Next Steps

### 1. Google Business Profile (Critical)
You need a verified Google Business Profile for San Miguel de Allende. This is non-negotiable for local search. Without it, you're invisible for "near me" searches and the local map pack. Add:
- Business category: "Web design agency" + "AI consultant" + "Marketing agency"
- Service area: San Miguel de Allende, Querétaro, Guanajuato, León
- Photos of your work (project screenshots, office if applicable)
- Posts with links to blog content

### 2. Location-Specific Landing Pages
Create two landing pages on untold.works:
- **/san-miguel-de-allende** — Targets SMA keywords, references local case studies, mentions restaurants/hotels/expat businesses
- **/queretaro** — Targets Querétaro keywords, references enterprise/professional services work, mentions the industrial corridor

These pages don't need to be long. 500-800 words each, optimized for 5-6 keywords, with links to your relevant case studies and blog posts.

### 3. Blog Posts That Rank for Specific Keywords
Your WhatsApp AI blog post is already going to pull in several of these keywords. Additional quick-win posts:
- "Why Your Restaurant in San Miguel Needs More Than a Facebook Page" (targets restaurant + SMA keywords)
- "The Business Owner's Guide to AI Automation in Querétaro" (targets automation + Querétaro keywords)

### 4. Schema Markup
Add LocalBusiness schema to your site with:
- Address in San Miguel de Allende
- Service area covering Querétaro and Guanajuato states
- Services offered in both languages
- This directly supports your AEO strategy too — AI engines pull structured data

### 5. Who You're Beating
The local competition in SMA is Kulander (hasn't updated their site design in years), SanMiguel10 (basic template shop), and MetaWeb (generic). In Querétaro, there are more players but NONE of them are selling AI-native websites or automation. You're not competing with them on price. You're competing in a category they don't even occupy.
