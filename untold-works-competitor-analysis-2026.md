# Untold.works Competitor Analysis
**Prepared: March 16, 2026**

---

## Your Positioning Summary

Untold.works is a **boutique AI agency** based in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico, serving clients across Mexico and the US. You operate at the intersection of four verticals: professional services websites, music industry solutions, small business/hospitality tools, and enterprise automation. Your stack centers on **n8n, Python, Claude/OpenAI, WhatsApp automation**, and custom web builds (React/Vite). You're bilingual (EN/ES), premium-priced ($5K-$100K+), and positioned as an integrated ecosystem builder rather than a point-solution vendor.

What makes you unique is the **cross-vertical range** combined with deep AI/automation chops. Most competitors specialize in only one of your pillars. Nobody else is combining music industry + hospitality + enterprise RevOps + bilingual Mexico/US service under one roof.

## Key Takeaways

**Your biggest competitive advantages:**
1. **Cross-vertical ecosystem approach** - Nobody else combines websites + automation + creative + strategy under one roof with this tech stack
2. **Bilingual Mexico/US positioning** - Very few AI automation agencies serve both markets fluently
3. **Music industry specialization** - Blue Note, Spotify pipeline work gives you credibility nobody in the automation agency space can match
4. **Enterprise depth + SMB accessibility** - You span $5K solo projects to $100K+ enterprise transformations (like Noxguard)

**Your biggest competitive threats:**
1. **GoHighLevel agencies** - Thousands of them targeting the same SMB/professional services clients with cheaper, template-driven solutions
2. **Visito AI** - Already proven in Mexican hospitality with WhatsApp automation. Your hospitality clients might try Visito before hiring you
3. **Goodspeed & N8N Lab** - Established n8n agency brands that will show up when prospects search for "n8n automation agency"
4. **SymphonyOS** - Capturing the "AI music marketing" narrative at $20/mo. Your music clients will encounter them

**Strategic gaps to exploit:**
1. Nobody is doing **AI automation + music industry + bilingual** together. Own this niche hard.
2. The Mexico-based AI agency space is underserved relative to demand (5M+ businesses need AI per market estimates)
3. Enterprise clients like Noxguard and Billiard Factory are your best case studies for differentiation - lean into the "we transformed the entire business" narrative vs. agencies that just build workflows
4. Your WhatsApp automation expertise + Mexico location is a natural fit as WhatsApp is the dominant messaging platform in LATAM (90%+ penetration)
