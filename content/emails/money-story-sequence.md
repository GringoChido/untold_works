## Email 2 — "Own vs. rent: the math nobody talks about" (The Framework)

**Subject:** The subscription trap (and how to escape it)
**Preview text:** SaaS costs go up every year. Owned systems cost $0/month forever.

---

SaaS companies have one goal: maximize your lifetime value.

That means your bill goes up every year. New "tiers." New "features" you didn't ask for behind higher paywalls. Price hikes buried in terms updates.

Here's the mental model most businesses never consider:

**Renting:** You pay monthly. Costs increase. If you stop paying, you lose everything. You never build equity in your tools.

**Owning:** You invest once. The system is yours. Monthly cost: $0. If you want to change providers, you take your system with you.

It's the same logic as buying vs. renting a house — except with business tools, the math is even more compelling because breakeven happens in months, not decades.

We build systems businesses own outright. No per-seat fees. No annual renewals. No vendor lock-in.
