### 1. The Setup — What the client was running

### 2. The Problem Nobody Sees
- Data siloed across 8 platforms
- Manual processes to move data between tools
- Team spending 10+ hours/week on admin
- No unified view of customer journey

### 3. What We Built
- One integrated platform: website + CRM + email + booking + analytics + automation
- AI-powered follow-ups and lead routing
- Bilingual content management (EN/ES)
- Team dashboard with role-based access

### 4. The Migration — How We Did It Without Downtime
- Parallel operation during transition
- Data migration strategy
- Team training approach
- 30-day support window

### 5. The Results
- Total cost: $10,000 one-time investment
- Monthly cost: $0
- Breakeven: 11 months
- 3-year savings: ~$17,000–$26,000
- 5-year savings: ~$35,000–$50,000
- Operational efficiency: +35%

### 6. The Ownership Difference
- What happens when you cancel SaaS: you lose everything
- What happens with an owned system: it's yours forever
- No price hikes. No per-seat fees. No vendor lock-in.
