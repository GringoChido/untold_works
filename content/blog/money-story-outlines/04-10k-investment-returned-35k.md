## Headline
How a $10K Investment Returned $35K in Savings Over 3 Years

## Sections

### 1. The Starting Point
- Small business spending ~$800/month on SaaS tools (~$9,600/year)
- Tools: Shopify ($299/mo), HubSpot ($150/mo), Mailchimp ($100/mo), Hootsuite ($99/mo), Zapier ($70/mo), Calendly ($16/mo), analytics ($50/mo)
- None integrated. Data siloed. Manual workarounds everywhere.

### 2. The Investment
- One-time build: $10,000
- What was included: brand, website, CRM, email, booking, analytics, AI automation
- Timeline: 8 weeks from kickoff to handoff
- Training: video library + live walkthrough + 30-day support

### 3. Year-by-Year Financial Trajectory
- **Year 1:** $10,000 invested. $9,600 saved in cancelled SaaS. Net: -$400 (nearly breakeven)
- **Year 2:** $0 cost. $9,600 saved. Cumulative net: +$9,200
- **Year 3:** $0 cost. $9,600 saved. Cumulative net: +$18,800
- Factor in SaaS price increases (10%/yr): actual 3-year savings = ~$31,000–$35,000

### 4. The Revenue Side
- Integrated system increased lead conversion (CRM + automated follow-ups)
- Estimate: 10–20% increase in booked appointments
- At $500 average customer value, even 2 extra customers/month = $12,000/year additional revenue

### 5. The Compound Effect
- Savings grow every year (SaaS prices increase, your cost stays $0)
- Revenue impact grows as automations optimize over time
- Total 3-year financial impact (savings + revenue): $35,000–$50,000

### 6. What This Means for Your Business
- The question isn't "can I afford a custom system?"
- The question is "can I afford not to own one?"
- Every month of inaction = another month of renting
