# Google Business Profile Setup Guide
**Untold.works — Step-by-Step**

---

## Why This Matters

Without a Google Business Profile (GBP), you don't show up in the local map pack when someone in San Miguel searches "web design near me" or "agencia de diseño web." That map pack gets 42% of all clicks in local search results. Right now your competitors Kulander, SanMiguel10, and MetaWeb are in there. You're not.

---

## Step 1: Go to Google Business Profile

Go to: **[business.google.com](https://business.google.com)**

Sign in with whatever Google account you want to manage the listing from. Use a business email if you have one, not a personal Gmail (cleaner for the long run).

---

## Step 2: Enter Your Business Name

**Business name:** `Untold Works`

Use the exact name your customers know. Don't keyword-stuff it (like "Untold Works - AI Web Design Agency San Miguel de Allende"). Google penalizes that and can suspend your listing.

---

## Step 3: Choose Your Business Type

Select: **"Online retail"** or **"Service business"**

Since you don't have a physical storefront customers walk into, choose the option that says you **deliver services to customers at their location** or that you're a **service-area business**. This is critical — it means Google won't require you to display a street address publicly, but you'll still appear in local results.

**Important 2026 update:** Google's verification flow now asks you to select your business type upfront, and selecting the wrong one can trigger a suspension. Choose "Service business" — NOT "storefront" unless you have a physical office clients visit.

---

## Step 4: Select Your Primary Category

**Primary category:** `Website designer`

This is the single most important ranking factor for local search. "Website designer" is more specific than "Marketing agency" and matches the exact search intent of people looking for web design in your area.

**Additional categories to add (up to 9):**
- `Internet marketing service`
- `Marketing agency`
- `Marketing consultant`
- `Software company`
- `Business to business service`
- `Consultant`

Don't add all 9 just to add them. Each one should genuinely represent a service you offer. The ones above all map to your actual services.

---

## Step 5: Set Your Service Area

Since you're a service-area business (you go to clients, they don't come to you), you'll set **service areas** instead of displaying an address.

**Add these service areas:**
- San Miguel de Allende, Guanajuato
- Querétaro, Querétaro
- León, Guanajuato
- Guanajuato (state)
- Querétaro (state)

Google's guideline says your service area shouldn't extend beyond about 2 hours of driving time. SMA to Querétaro is ~1 hour, SMA to León is ~1.5 hours. You're well within bounds.

**Your address:** You'll still need to enter a physical address during setup for verification purposes, but you can choose to **hide it** from customers. Use your actual business address in San Miguel de Allende. It won't be displayed publicly.

---

## Step 6: Add Contact Information

**Phone number:** Your business WhatsApp number or main phone line. A Mexican number (+52) is ideal for local trust.

**Website:** `https://untold.works`

---

## Step 7: Verification

Google needs to verify you're a real business. In 2026, the most common verification methods for service-area businesses in Mexico are:

**Video verification (most likely):** Google will ask you to record a short video showing:
- Your business signage or branding (laptop sticker, business cards, branded materials count)
- Your work environment
- Proof you operate in the area (mail with your address, lease, utility bill)
- Your face (they want to see a real person)

**Tips for video verification:**
- Have your business cards or branded materials visible
- Show your laptop with untold.works open
- Show a piece of mail or official document with your SMA address
- Keep it under 5 minutes, be natural
- If you have a home office, that's fine — show it

**Other possible methods:**
- Postcard (Google mails a postcard with a PIN to your address — takes 1-2 weeks in Mexico, sometimes longer)
- Phone call or text verification (less common for new listings)

**Verification timeline:** Can take anywhere from a few days to 2-3 weeks. Start now.

---

## Step 8: Complete Your Profile (Do All of This)

Once verified, fill out EVERYTHING. Completeness directly affects ranking.

### Business Description (750 characters max)

**English version:**
```
Untold Works is a creative technology agency in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico. We build AI-native business platforms — bilingual websites, automated workflows, CRM systems, and WhatsApp AI for businesses across Mexico and the US. We work with restaurants, hotels, professional services, retailers, and enterprise clients. Founded by Joshua Semolik. Services in English and Spanish.
```

**Note:** GBP descriptions aren't heavily weighted for ranking, but they show up in your listing and help customers understand what you do. Keep it factual, no promotional language (Google rejects "best" or "#1" claims).

### Services

Add each service as a separate item with a description:

1. **Website Design & Development**
   - Custom bilingual websites for businesses in Mexico and the US. React, mobile-first, SEO-optimized.

2. **AI Automation & Workflows**
   - Custom AI workflow automation using n8n, Claude, and OpenAI. WhatsApp AI, CRM integration, lead routing.

3. **WhatsApp AI Systems**
   - AI-powered WhatsApp agents for restaurants, hotels, and businesses. Voice note transcription, bilingual, payment integration.

4. **Brand Identity & Strategy**
   - Logo, visual identity, core messaging, and positioning for businesses ready to scale.

5. **E-Commerce & Shopify**
   - Online stores on Shopify and custom platforms. ERP integration, inventory management, omnichannel.

6. **Revenue Operations**
   - CRM setup, lead qualification, sales automation, attribution modeling for growth-stage businesses.

### Business Hours

Set your actual working hours. If you're flexible, something like:
- Monday–Friday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Saturday: By appointment
- Sunday: Closed

### Attributes

Google lets you add attributes. Select all that apply:
- "Identifies as Latino-owned" (if applicable)
- "Online appointments"
- "Online estimates"
- Languages: English, Spanish

## Step 9: Add Photos (Critical for Clicks)

Profiles with photos get 42% more requests for directions and 35% more website clicks. Add:

- **Logo** — Your Untold.works logo
- **Cover photo** — Something that represents your work (a project screenshot, your workspace, SMA street scene)
- **Work photos** — 5-10 screenshots of websites you've built, dashboards, workflow diagrams
- **Team photo** — A photo of you (people hire people)

Update photos monthly. Google rewards active profiles.

---

## Step 10: First Google Post

Once verified, publish your first Google Post immediately. This signals to Google that the profile is active.

**Suggested first post:**
```
We just published a complete guide to WhatsApp AI for Mexican businesses — covering API setup, voice note handling, OXXO/SPEI payments, bilingual configuration, and real implementation timelines. Read it on our blog at untold.works. Available in English and Spanish.
```

Then post weekly. Share your blog posts, project highlights, or quick tips. Google Posts expire after 7 days, so consistency matters.

---

## Step 11: Get Your First Reviews

Reviews are the second biggest ranking factor for local search after your primary category. You need them.

**Immediate actions:**
- Message 5 past clients and ask for a Google review. Send them the direct review link (you'll find this in your GBP dashboard under "Ask for reviews")
- After every new project, send a WhatsApp message with the review link
- Respond to every review — positive or negative — within 24 hours

**Target:** 5 reviews in your first month, 10-15 within 3 months. In SMA, most of your competitors have fewer than 20 total. You can catch up fast.

---

## Step 12: Ongoing Maintenance

GBP is not "set it and forget it." Active profiles rank higher.

**Weekly:**
- Publish a Google Post (blog link, project update, tip)
- Respond to any new reviews or questions

**Monthly:**
- Add 2-3 new photos
- Check your insights (who's finding you, what they're searching)
- Update services if you've added new offerings

**Quarterly:**
- Audit your categories (are they still the right ones?)
- Check competitor profiles (what categories are they using?)
- Update your business description if your positioning has evolved

---

## What to Expect

**Week 1-2:** Verification process. Submit everything and wait.

**Week 2-4:** Profile goes live. You start appearing in local search for your primary category + service area.

**Month 1-3:** As you accumulate reviews and posts, your ranking improves. You'll start showing up in the map pack for searches like "diseño web San Miguel de Allende" and "website designer near me."

**Month 3-6:** With consistent posting, reviews, and the landing pages we're about to build, you should be in the top 3 map pack results for most of your target keywords in SMA. Querétaro will take longer due to higher competition, but the AI-specific keywords will be yours quickly.
